# VoiceTecx Privacy Policy

Effective [TO FILL: date] · TECXMATE COMPANY LIMITED ([TO FILL: registered address]; tax ID [TO FILL])

VoiceTecx turns speech into text **on your Mac**. This page says exactly what the app and our shop do with your data.

## 1. Your voice and text stay on your Mac

- Speech is recognised by models running on your Mac. Audio is sent only to VoiceTecx's own engines on the same Mac
  (never through a proxy) and is **never sent to us or anyone else**.
- **Recordings are not kept.** The audio of a dictation is discarded once it is turned into text.
- **History** (the text of each dictation, the app it went into and the time) is stored only on your Mac, in a file
  only your user account can read, for as long as you choose in Settings (default 30 days). You can delete it at any
  time.
- **Clipboard history** (if you turn it on) keeps your last 30 copied items on your Mac; items that password managers
  mark as concealed are never kept.
- 常用詞 (vocabulary), corrections and settings stay on your Mac.
- **No account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting.** VoiceTecx does not track how you use it.

## 2. When the app connects to the internet

Only in these cases:
1. **Downloading a language** when you click Download: from huggingface.co (Hugging Face, Inc.). Every file is checked
   against a fingerprint built into the app.
2. **Checking for updates** (Sparkle), only after the app has asked you whether to check automatically, or when you
   choose 檢查更新⋯ / Check for Updates. No system profile is sent.
3. **購買授權⋯ / Buy a Licence** opens our website in your browser; **回報問題 / Report a Problem** opens a draft in
   your own mail app, which you can read and edit, and nothing is sent until you send it.

For 1 and 2, as with any download, Hugging Face receives your IP address and the app's name and version in the
request. We do not receive or combine this information.

## 3. Your licence

A licence key contains the **name and email address** you gave when buying, the plan, the number of seats and the
version it covers. It is checked on your Mac with no connection to us and is stored only on your Mac.

## 4. When you buy

- **In Taiwan** payment is processed by **ECPay** (綠界科技), which also issues the electronic uniform invoice
  (電子發票). **Outside Taiwan** our reseller **Paddle** (Paddle.com Market Ltd) is the merchant of record and processes
  payment and tax. We never see or store your full card number.
- We keep: your name, email, order number, plan, the licence key issued, and the invoice records the law requires.
- Purposes: delivering and supporting your licence, refunds, invoicing and accounting, and answering your messages.
- Retention: for as long as your licence is supported, and invoices and accounting records for the period Taiwan's
  tax law requires.
- We do not sell or share your data, except with ECPay or Paddle to process the payment, and with authorities
  where the law requires.

## 5. Your rights

Under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act (個人資料保護法) you may ask to see, copy, correct, or delete your data, or
ask us to stop using it, except where the law requires us to keep it. If you do not give a name and email, we cannot
issue a licence. Write to **support@tecxmate.com**.

## 6. Changes

If the app's handling of data changes, we will update this page before that version is released, and note the
change in the release notes.
